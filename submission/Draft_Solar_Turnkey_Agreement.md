## 2. SCOPE OF TURNKEY WORK

The Contractor shall execute the installation on a complete turnkey basis, encompassing:
1. **Engineering & Site Design**: Rooftop structural load verification, electrical layout design, and shadow analysis.
2. **Supply of Approved Equipment**:
   - **Solar PV Modules**: Tier-1 Domestic Content Requirement (DCR) compliant monocrystalline PERC / TOPCon modules listed on the MNRE Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM), with a minimum wattage of 540 Wp per module.
   - **Grid-Tied Inverter**: High-efficiency string inverter (IP65 rated) with built-in Wi-Fi monitoring and minimum 10-year manufacturer replacement warranty.
   - **Mounting Structure**: Hot-Dip Galvanized (HDG, minimum 80-micron coating) structure engineered to withstand wind speeds up to 150 km/h.
   - **Balance of System (BOS)**: Polycab/Finolex UV-resistant solar DC/AC cables, IP67 MC4 connectors, dedicated ACDB and DCDB boxes equipped with Type-II Surge Protection Devices (SPD) and Miniature Circuit Breakers (MCB).
   - **Earthing & Lightning Protection**: Three (3) dedicated chemical earthing pits (Inverter, DC array, Lightning) and an early streamer emission (ESE) / copper lightning arrester complying with CEA safety regulations.
3. **MSEDCL Net Metering Liaisoning**: Preparing, submitting, and tracking the grid-tied net-metering application on the MSEDCL Mahavitaran online portal; coordinating site inspection with the MSEDCL Junior Engineer; and installing the bi-directional smart net meter.
4. **National Portal Subsidy Claim Submission**: Submitting the commissioning report, joint inspection report, and customer bank details on the PM Surya Ghar National Portal for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) disbursement.

## 4. GOVERNMENT SUBSIDY (PM SURYA GHAR) TERMS

1. The Customer acknowledges that the Central Government Subsidy under the **PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana** is disbursed via **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** directly into the Customer’s Aadhaar-linked bank account by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE).
2. As of the date of this Agreement, the active subsidy rates are:
   - ₹30,000 per kW for systems up to 2 kW;
   - ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW;
   - Capped at a maximum of **₹78,000** for systems $\ge$ 3 kW.
3. The Contractor shall diligently prepare, file, and track the subsidy dossier on the National Portal. However, the final release of funds remains subject to central government treasury schedules. The Customer shall provide a cancelled cheque and Aadhaar linkage in a timely manner.

## 5. GENERATION ASSURANCE & WARRANTIES

### 5.1 Solar Generation Benchmark:
Under normal meteorological conditions and unshaded rooftop exposure in the Vidarbha region, the system is engineered to generate approximately **1,400 units (kWh) per kW per year** during Year 1, with a maximum annual degradation rate of **0.7%** thereafter.

### 5.2 Equipment Warranties:
- **Solar Modules**: 25-Year Linear Performance Warranty (at least 80% output at Year 25) and 12-Year Product Workmanship Warranty.
- **Inverter**: 10-Year Comprehensive Manufacturer Replacement Warranty.
- **Mounting Structure**: 10-Year Structural Integrity Warranty against corrosion.
- **Contractor Workmanship**: 5-Year Turnkey Installation Workmanship & Operations Warranty.

## 6. OBLIGATIONS OF THE CUSTOMER

The Customer shall:
1. Provide unhindered, safe access to the rooftop for the Contractor's personnel and material transport during installation hours (08:00 to 18:00).
2. Supply temporary single-phase electricity and water required for civil works and system testing at no charge to the Contractor.
3. Ensure that the rooftop remains free of newly constructed obstructions, hoardings, or shade-casting structures for the lifetime of the system.
4. Keep the active MSEDCL electricity account in good standing without outstanding tariff arrears.
